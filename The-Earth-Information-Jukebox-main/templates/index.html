A NEW PERSPECTIVE

What if you could hear our planet?

Earth's condition is usually communicated through images: drought maps, temperature anomalies, precipitation records, satellite observations, and many other forms of scientific data. But what if we could experience this information through sound?

EARTH TUNE transforms environmental observations into sound, creating another way to explore scientific information. By combining visual and auditory experiences, the project also aims to make Earth data more accessible to people with visual impairments.

SCIENCE & ACCESSIBILITY

Making science accessible to everyone

NASA has explored the relationship between science and sound through initiatives such as its Universe of Sound program. Research published in 2024 in Frontiers in Communication also explored how sonification can support learning and accessibility.

EARTH TUNE builds on this idea: scientific information should be experienced through more than one sense.
